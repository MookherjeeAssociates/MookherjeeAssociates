### Welcome to Mookherjee Associates 👋

**Mookherjee Associates** is an independent legal, tax, and corporate consultancy firm based in West Bengal, India. Our practice provides comprehensive income tax compliance, GST filings, property registration, corporate structuring, tax audit representation, and precision legal drafting.

##### 🏛️ Core Practice Areas & Services
* **Civil Litigation:** Drafting and pleading for Civil Suits, Property Disputes, Money Recovery Suits, and Consumer Forum cases.
* **Property & Real Estate:** Property Registration, Deed Drafting, **Mutation**, and handling Municipal/Land reform disputes.
* **Startup & Registrations:** One-stop shop for Trade License, P-Tax, MSME Registration, FSSAI, and Import-Export Code (IEC).
* **Taxation & Compliance:** Income tax consultancy (ITR filing, Scrutiny, Appeals) and GST & Indirect Tax solutions.
* **Corporate & Commercial Law:** Company incorporation (Pvt Ltd, LLP), annual ROC compliances, and MCA filings.
